Our Story

From the Farm to Your Plate

Founded in 2018 by Chef Marcus Hale, Ember & Oak began as a modest farm stand with a wood-fired oven and a dream. Today, it stands as one of the region's most celebrated dining destinations.

Every morning, our team visits local farms and fisheries to hand-select the freshest ingredients. We believe that great food starts with respect for the land and the people who tend it.

"Cooking is not about recipes. It's about understanding ingredients, respecting tradition, and having the courage to create something new."

— Chef Marcus Hale, Founder
